# Reviewer access

Stockmarket TV is a shared game board, controlled by a standard D-pad remote. It is not a financial trading service. All balances, shares and transactions are fictional. There is no real-money wagering, cash prize, payment, subscription or advertising.

## Explore without an account

From the TV home screen select **Preview game** to view a clearly labelled sample game with six players, stock prices, portfolios and activity. Select Back to return to the home screen. This preview does not create a live lobby or demonstrate the complete multiplayer flow.

Select **Privacy & support** for the full policy and support email. Focus the policy text and use Up/Down to scroll. Back returns home; Back again exits the app.

## Complete multiplayer flow

1. On the TV choose 6 periods and select Create game. A QR code and lobby code appear.
2. Scan the QR code on a phone, or open the displayed game URL in a phone browser. Sign in to a player account. The TV itself has no account or login.
3. Join enough players to fill at least four seats. The phone host can add AI players through the companion website; human participants use their phones to make trading decisions. The game supports four to six seats in total.
4. Launch the game from the creating TV. Trading and private insights stay on phones. The TV shows public market data and action animations.
5. At market close, advance the public reveals using the TV remote. Resolve any pending hold-stock decisions on the relevant player's phone.
6. The creating TV selects Next Period or Finish Game after the summary. Final results show rankings, portfolios and price histories.
7. Exit and reopen the app to revisit a game from Recent games. Watching a game from its code does not grant control over it.
